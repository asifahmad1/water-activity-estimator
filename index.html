4 · Outputs

Honest scope. Estimates come from published model equations with typical parameters (~25 °C). Real products need their own measured isotherm: parameters are temperature-specific, and hysteresis is not modelled (adsorption and desorption curves differ). The blend prediction assumes a sealed system, full equilibration, no ingredient interactions beyond water transfer, and no temperature change. This tool is not a substitute for a measured aw (dew-point / chilled-mirror meter) in commercial release decisions. Everything runs in this page — no data leaves your browser.
